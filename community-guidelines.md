_Effective: September 21, 2026_

Pocket KGS provides access to public rooms, game discussions, private messages, profiles, avatars, and other content created by KGS users. Treat other people with respect and remember that KGS users may differ widely in age, language, culture, and Go experience.

These guidelines supplement the [Pocket KGS Terms of Use]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}) and the [KGS Terms of Service](https://www.gokgs.com/tos.jsp).

## Do not post or promote

- Child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, or any sexualization of minors.
- Pornography, sexual solicitation, or sexually explicit content.
- Threats, encouragement of violence, self-harm content, or graphic violence.
- Hate speech or attacks based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, or another protected characteristic.
- Harassment, bullying, stalking, doxxing, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Another person's private information, private messages, image, or voice without an appropriate right or consent.
- Fraud, scams, spam, malicious links, malware, or deceptive impersonation.
- Illegal content or instructions intended to facilitate serious wrongdoing.
- Cheating, match manipulation, abuse of ranking systems, or disruption of games and rooms.
- Content or conduct prohibited by KGS or Google Play policies.

Keep game and room discussions relevant, avoid flooding, and follow reasonable instructions from KGS administrators.

## Block unwanted interaction

Use the **Censor** control on a user's Pocket KGS profile to hide supported content and prevent supported direct interactions from that user. You can reverse this control later. You can also limit new private conversations in Pocket KGS settings.

## Report abuse or objectionable content

Pocket KGS does not currently operate a separate moderation service. KGS administrators control KGS-hosted content and accounts.

To report conduct on KGS:

1. In Pocket KGS, use **Report this player** on a player profile or **Report** beside a room, game, private-chat, or saved message. Add a short factual explanation. Pocket KGS privately sends the report and relevant context through AdminBot to the KGS admin team.
2. You may also contact an available KGS administrator or assistant in a private KGS message. Administrators are identified by a star in KGS user lists.
3. If no administrator is available, email a description of what happened to [admin@gokgs.com](mailto:admin@gokgs.com), as directed by the KGS Terms of Service.
4. Preserve the username, approximate time, room or game, and a short factual description. Do not repost harmful material publicly.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Reports involving child sexual abuse material should also be made to the appropriate national reporting authority; do not download, copy, or attach illegal material.

Use the public Pocket KGS issue tracker only to report a defect in the app's blocking or safety tools. Do not paste private messages, personal information, or objectionable material into a public issue.

## Enforcement

KGS is responsible for investigating and enforcing conduct rules on its service. The Pocket KGS developer may restrict access to app-specific support spaces, close public issues, preserve evidence, or cooperate with KGS and lawful authorities when appropriate.
