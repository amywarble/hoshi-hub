KGS Nova is an independent desktop client for the [KGS Go Server](https://www.gokgs.com/), available for Windows, macOS, and Linux. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by KGS.

The separate [Pocket KGS pages]({% include page-url.html key='android/home' %}) cover the Android app.

For desktop bugs and feature requests, [open a public GitHub issue](https://github.com/amywarble/hoshi-hub/issues/new/choose) and identify the app as **KGS Nova desktop**. Do not include credentials, private messages, or other sensitive information.
