# KGS Nova desktop help

For a reproducible bug or a feature request, search the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/amywarble/pocket-kgs-support/issues), then [report a desktop bug](https://github.com/amywarble/pocket-kgs-support/issues/new?template=desktop_bug_report.md) or [request a desktop feature](https://github.com/amywarble/pocket-kgs-support/issues/new?template=desktop_feature_request.md). Include the app version, your operating system and processor architecture, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce the problem.

The desktop **About → Report an app issue** button opens a draft with version, operating system, architecture, and runtime details. You can edit or remove those details before submitting. The button does not submit the report for you.

Screenshots and logs can help, but review and redact them first. Never post passwords, access tokens, email addresses, private messages, private game or room content, or unreviewed logs. Desktop logs may contain usernames and protocol data.

For audio problems on macOS or Linux, include whether playback, microphone capture, or both are affected. Check your system audio settings and microphone permissions. Saving passwords on Linux requires an available Secret Service keyring.

## KGS help and private concerns

For account recovery, account status, server-side data, or moderation, contact a KGS administrator or [admin@gokgs.com](mailto:admin@gokgs.com).

- Read the [desktop Privacy Policy]({{ '/desktop/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
- Follow the [desktop Community Guidelines]({{ '/desktop/community-guidelines/' | relative_url }}) for blocking and reporting.
- See [Account and Data Deletion]({{ '/desktop/account-and-data-deletion/' | relative_url }}) for removing local data or requesting KGS account deletion.
- Report vulnerabilities through the [private security-reporting form](https://github.com/amywarble/pocket-kgs-support/security/advisories/new).
- Email the developer at [hoshihatchery@gmail.com](mailto:hoshihatchery@gmail.com) for private app, privacy, or sensitive safety concerns.

Do not use public issues for account details, deletion requests, sensitive safety reports, or security vulnerabilities.
