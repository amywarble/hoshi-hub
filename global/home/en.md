Hoshi Hub provides documentation, downloads, support, and policies for clients of the [KGS Go Server](https://www.gokgs.com/). Pocket KGS, KGS Nova, and Mobi are officially endorsed by KGS and the American Go Association (AGA).

{% include client-grid.html %}

For app bugs and feature requests, [open a public GitHub issue](https://github.com/amywarble/hoshi-hub/issues/new/choose).
Do not include passwords, email addresses, private messages, access tokens, or other sensitive information.
