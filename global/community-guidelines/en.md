_Effective: October 27, 2026_

These Community Guidelines apply to Pocket KGS and KGS Nova. Both clients display rooms, game discussions, private messages, profiles, avatars, and other KGS user content. Treat other people with respect, regardless of age, language, culture, or Go experience.

These guidelines supplement the [Pocket KGS · Terms of Use]({% include page-url.html key='android/terms' %}), the [KGS Nova · Terms of Use]({% include page-url.html key='desktop/terms' %}), and the [KGS Terms of Service](https://www.gokgs.com/tos.jsp).

## Do not post or promote

- Child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, or any sexualization of minors.
- Pornography, sexual solicitation, or sexually explicit content.
- Threats, encouragement of violence, self-harm content, or graphic violence.
- Hate speech or attacks based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, or another protected characteristic.
- Harassment, bullying, stalking, doxxing, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Another person's private information, private messages, image, or voice without an appropriate right or consent.
- Fraud, scams, spam, malicious links, malware, or deceptive impersonation.
- Illegal content or instructions intended to facilitate serious wrongdoing.
- Cheating, match manipulation, abuse of ranking systems, or disruption of games and rooms.
- Content or conduct prohibited by KGS policies.

Keep game and room discussions relevant, avoid flooding, and follow reasonable instructions from KGS administrators.

## Report abuse or objectionable content

KGS administrators manage KGS-hosted content and accounts. Privately contact an available KGS administrator or assistant, or email [admin@gokgs.com](mailto:admin@gokgs.com). Include the username, approximate time, room or game, and a short factual description. Do not repost harmful material publicly.

For the controls in your app, see [Pocket KGS]({% include page-url.html key='android/community-guidelines' %}) or [KGS Nova]({% include page-url.html key='desktop/community-guidelines' %}).

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Reports involving child sexual abuse material should also be made to the appropriate national reporting authority; do not download, copy, or attach illegal material.

## Enforcement

KGS is responsible for investigating and enforcing conduct rules on its service. The client developer may restrict access to app-specific support spaces, close public issues, preserve evidence, or cooperate with KGS and lawful authorities when appropriate.
