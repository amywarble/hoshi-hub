Follow the [shared Community Guidelines]({% include page-url.html key='global/community-guidelines' %}), which apply to both Pocket KGS and KGS Nova, together with this app’s [Terms of Use]({% include page-url.html key='android/terms' %}).

## Blocking and reporting in Pocket KGS

Use the **Censor** control on a user's Pocket KGS profile to hide supported content and prevent supported direct interactions from that user. You can reverse this control later. You can also limit new private conversations in Pocket KGS settings.

Open **Get Help** and send a short factual message to AdminBot for the KGS administrators. Include the username, approximate time, room or game, and what happened. You can add more information in the conversation with AdminBot. Check the delivery feedback; if you cannot send the message, contact an administrator directly or email [admin@gokgs.com](mailto:admin@gokgs.com).

For Pocket KGS on Android, content and conduct must also comply with Google Play policies.
