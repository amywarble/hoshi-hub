_Effective: October 27, 2026_

These guidelines apply to anyone operating a bot through Mobi. You are responsible for your bots, their engines, and their activity on KGS, including activity while you are away. These rules supplement the [Mobi Terms of Use]({% include page-url.html key='mobi/terms' %}) and the [KGS Terms of Service](https://www.gokgs.com/tos.jsp).

## Be honest about automated accounts

- Identify bot accounts as automated players. Do not use a bot to impersonate a human, disguise automated play as human play, or make the service appear to have human activity it does not have.
- Do not impersonate another person, organization, KGS administrator, or official service.
- Obtain any permissions required by KGS. Ranked bot play requires administrator approval; see the [KGS Bot FAQ](https://www.gokgs.com/help/faq/bots.html). Do not bypass account restrictions or use replacement accounts to evade moderation.

## Do not operate bot swarms

- Do not run fleets of bot accounts to crowd rooms, fill game lists, dominate matchmaking, manufacture activity, or overwhelm other players or the server.
- Coordinate with KGS administrators before operating multiple simultaneous bot accounts or conducting large-scale tests. Administrator approval does not permit disruptive behavior.
- Limit connections, game offers, retries, and reconnects to what your bot needs. Do not flood chat, repeatedly challenge users who decline, or use account rotation to evade limits.

## Keep play fair

- Do not manipulate rankings through arranged wins or losses, self-play between your accounts, collusion, or selective disconnections. Use free games for testing that is not suitable for rated play.
- Do not use Mobi to provide concealed engine assistance to a human in a game where that assistance is prohibited.
- Use accurate game offers and settings. Do not lure opponents into games with misleading descriptions or conditions.
- Respect opponents, their privacy, room rules, and administrator instructions. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, advertise, or distribute illegal, abusive, or malicious content through account names, profiles, game descriptions, or automated messages.

## Operate responsibly

Test your engine and configuration before unattended play. Choose time settings your hardware can sustain, monitor failures and unfinished games, and stop a bot that repeatedly disconnects, stalls, or behaves incorrectly. Recovery features do not remove your responsibility to supervise the bot.

Keep credentials private and use only accounts you are authorized to control. Protect retained logs and game information; do not publish private communications or personal information without permission. Follow the [Privacy Policy]({% include page-url.html key='global/privacy' %}) and [Account and Data Deletion]({% include page-url.html key='global/account-and-data-deletion' %}) instructions when retiring a bot.

## Reports and enforcement

For KGS abuse or bot-account concerns, privately contact an available KGS administrator or email [admin@gokgs.com](mailto:admin@gokgs.com). Include the bot username, approximate time, room or game, and a factual description. Do not post passwords or private account details in public issues.

KGS administrators manage service access, account permissions, rankings, and moderation. The client developer can address Mobi defects and moderate app-support spaces but cannot reverse KGS moderation decisions. For non-sensitive software defects, [report a Mobi bug](https://github.com/amywarble/hoshi-hub/issues/new?template=mobi_bug_report.md); for vulnerabilities, follow the [Security Policy]({% include page-url.html key='global/security-policy' %}).
